# 03 — Problem Framing

> [!NOTE] INSTRUCTIONS
> State the problem as something that hurts a specific group today, not as a
> missing feature. If the "cost of doing nothing" row is not painful to read,
> the problem is not sharp enough yet. Delete this block once a stakeholder
> agrees with all four rows.

## The problem

Product prices and invoice totals are kept in a shared spreadsheet and written
onto invoices by hand. Both copies drift out of sync, and nobody notices until
a customer disputes a charge.

## Who is affected

| Group | How |
|---|---|
| Billing staff | Retype prices manually and cannot tell which copy is current |
| Catalog staff | Update one spreadsheet copy, unaware two others exist |
| Customers | Receive an invoice with the wrong price, then dispute it |
| Store manager | Answers disputes with no record of which price was correct |

## Cost of doing nothing

Every disputed invoice costs a staff member roughly 30 minutes to trace back
through spreadsheet history, and a price error found by a customer instead of
by staff damages trust before it is ever corrected.

## Success criterion

The problem is solved when a price exists in exactly one place, every invoice
reads that price at issue time, and a disputed invoice can be traced to its
source price in under one minute.
